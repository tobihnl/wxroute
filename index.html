How to read it
Each stop is scored at the hour you would actually be there, from your departure time and the average speed set for that route. Rain flags start at 0.6 mm/h and turn serious at 1.5. The colour follows what most models agree on, so a single wet outlier cannot set the verdict. The squares show every model, and the mm figure shows the wettest one after a slash when they disagree.
CAPE above 800 after 11:00 is the alpine convection signal. It says the atmosphere is willing, not that a cell will hit your road. That last part is a radar question.
